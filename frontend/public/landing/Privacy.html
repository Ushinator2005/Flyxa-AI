This policy applies to all Flyxa products and services. It explains what data we collect, the purposes we use it for, and the rights available to you. If anything is unclear, contact us at team@flyxa.app.

01Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all products, websites, and services operated by Flyxa ("Flyxa", "we", "us"). It describes how we collect, use, store, and disclose personal information, and the rights available to you. It applies whether you hold a free or paid account, are on our waitlist, or are simply visiting our website.

02Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: your email address and authentication credentials, collected when you create an account or join the waitlist.
  • Trading data: screenshots, trade records, journal entries, notes, and rule configurations you submit to the service.
  • Payment information: processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Communications: the contents of messages you send to our support channels.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage information: features accessed, pages viewed, and interaction events, used to maintain and improve the service.
  • Device and log data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and timestamps, collected for security and diagnostics.

03Cookies and similar technologies

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and measure aggregate usage of our website. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling essential cookies may prevent parts of the service from functioning.

04How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the service: importing trades, scoring your process, and generating analytical insights for your account.
  • To secure the service, including fraud prevention, abuse detection, and account authentication.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • To send transactional communications regarding your account, billing, and material changes to our terms or this policy.
  • To send product updates and announcements, only where you have expressly opted in. You may withdraw consent at any time via the unsubscribe link in any such email.
  • To analyse aggregated, de-identified usage patterns to improve product performance and reliability.

05AI processing of your data

Flyxa uses machine-learning models to read trade screenshots, score your execution, and identify behavioural patterns in your journal.

Your data trains nothing but your own insights. Content you submit is processed solely to generate outputs for your account. We do not use your trading data, journal entries, or screenshots to train models applied to other users, and we contractually prohibit third-party model providers from retaining or training on your data.

06Legal bases for processing

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we process personal data on the following bases: performance of a contract (providing the service you signed up for); legitimate interests (securing and improving the service, in ways that do not override your rights); consent (optional marketing communications, which you may withdraw at any time); and legal obligation (tax, accounting, and lawful requests).

07Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal data. We disclose personal information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: vetted vendors that host infrastructure, process payments, or deliver email on our behalf, bound by contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations, and only to the extent necessary to perform their function.
  • Legal requirements: where disclosure is required by law, regulation, or a valid legal process, or is necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of Flyxa or others.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your data and you will be notified of any change of controller.

We never disclose your trading history to brokers, proprietary trading firms, or other traders, and we do not use your journal content for advertising or profiling.

08Data retention

  • Account and trading data: retained for as long as your account remains active.
  • Deleted accounts: all associated personal data is permanently erased within 30 days of a confirmed deletion request, except where longer retention is required by law (e.g. tax and accounting records).
  • Backups: encrypted backups are rotated on a fixed schedule; deleted data ages out of backups within 90 days.
  • Waitlist data: if you join the waitlist but never create an account, we retain your email only until launch communications conclude, or until you unsubscribe, whichever is earlier.

09Security

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and encrypted at rest using industry-standard algorithms. Access to production systems is restricted to authorised personnel, protected by multi-factor authentication, and logged for audit purposes. We review our security posture regularly and remediate identified vulnerabilities on prioritised timelines.

No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authority without undue delay, as required by applicable law (including the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme under the Privacy Act 1988).

10International data transfers

Our infrastructure providers may store or process data in jurisdictions other than your own. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision covering the receiving jurisdiction.

11Your rights

Subject to applicable law (including the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and, where applicable, the GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access: obtain a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: request permanent deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Portability: export your data in a structured, machine-readable format from account settings.
  • Objection and restriction: object to, or request restriction of, certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent: where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing.
  • Complaint: lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority (in Australia, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner; in the EU/UK, your local supervisory authority).

To exercise any of these rights, use account settings or contact team@flyxa.app. We verify all requests and respond within 30 days.

12Marketing choices

We send marketing emails only with your express opt-in. Every marketing email includes a one-click unsubscribe. Unsubscribing does not affect transactional emails necessary to operate your account (billing receipts, security notices, material policy changes).

13Children

The service is not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

14Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will always reflect the current version. Material changes will be notified to account holders by email with reasonable advance notice. Continued use of the service after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

15Contact us

For questions regarding this policy or our data practices, contact our privacy team at team@flyxa.app. We aim to respond to all privacy enquiries within two business days.
