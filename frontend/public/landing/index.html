From the founder
Most of my losing days were never my system; it was my psychology.

I knew my setups; I knew where to TP, when to trail, and I could tell you exactly what I had to do to follow my system. Then one red trade where I'm either unlucky or take a premature entry and a different person takes over, all the discipline and hard work of weeks slowly increasing my balance vanishes. I would start doubling my size, forcing entries and losing all the progress I had made for weeks in a matter of minutes.

The worst part wasn't losing. It was that every answer was already sitting in my own journal. The same mistake, the same sizing issue, the same emotional spiral, week after week. I just couldn't see it from the inside.

So I built Flyxa: the tool that reads my trading the way I never could. I use it every session. Now it's yours too.

Ushnish · Founder, FlyxaFutures & prop trader · Sydney

How do I get access? +
Join the waitlist and invites go out in order. Flyxa is in private beta and free for everyone with access, with no card required. Paid plans arrive after launch, and beta members will be the first to know.
Which platforms and brokers does Flyxa support? +
Any platform you can screenshot. Flyxa reads charts from TradingView, MT4/MT5, NinjaTrader, ThinkorSwim and others, and you can import trade history by CSV.
Is my trading data private and secure? +
Yes. Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit, never sold, and never shared. You can export or permanently delete everything at any time. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
Does it work with prop-firm rules? +
Yes. Set your daily loss limit, max contracts, trade count and trading window, and Flyxa checks every session against them. Presets exist for the common firms, and you can write your own rules for anything else.
What does Flyxa AI actually tell me? +
It reads your own journal, not the internet. You get a process score for each session, the behavioural patterns behind your losses, and one specific thing to fix, with the trades it drew that from so you can check its work.
I've quit every journal I've started. Why is this different? +
Because you barely do anything. Hit the shortcut, and about 15 seconds later the trade is logged, graded and analysed. The reading, tagging and pattern-finding that made journaling homework is Flyxa's job now, and red days are exactly the entries it learns the most from.
